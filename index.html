Story Reference: Biography of Pocahontas

1

Early Life in Virginia

Pocahontas was the daughter of Chief Powhatan. Her real name was Matoaka. As a child, she was curious, brave, and loved by her tribe.

2

Meeting the Settlers (1607)

English settlers arrived and built Jamestown. Young Pocahontas became a friend to Captain John Smith and helped bring peace.

3

A Kind Peacemaker

When the settlers had no food in winter, she brought corn and supplies to save them. She helped two different cultures understand each other.

4

Journey & Legacy

Later, she married John Rolfe, took the name Rebecca, and traveled to London. Today, we remember her for her courage and kindness.
